Supervision


I believe that academic supervision goes beyond guiding students through a research project; it is about supporting their intellectual growth, encouraging independent thinking, and helping them develop into confident researchers and professionals. My goal is to create an environment built on mutual respect, curiosity, critical thinking, and open dialogue, where students feel encouraged to explore new ideas, challenge assumptions, and pursue meaningful contributions to their fields.
